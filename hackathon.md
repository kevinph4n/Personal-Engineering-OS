#### Team Greeting Template:"Good [morning/afternoon] judges and everyone, we are team [Team Name]. Today, we are here to tackle a critical issue in [Target Industry/Field].
---
## **PHASE 1: The Hook, Problem & Demo (~3 minutes)**
#### 1/ **The Hook (Stats/Fact)**: Start with 1-2 shocking statistics or a pressing issue to grab the judges' attention in the very first seconds
#### 2/ **The Solution Abstract**: Briefly state your team's solution (e.g., "We built a platform to detect and analyze...")
#### 3/ **The Demo**: Introduce a real-world scenario (User Story) and jump straight into the live demo. Show the judges exactly how users interact with the product to solve the stated problem
---
## **PHASE 2: Tech Stack & Logic and Algorithm & Tech-to-Value (~2 minutes)**
#### 1/ **Tech Stack Overview**: Present the system architecture using a simple flow chart (Data flow -> Processing -> UI)
#### 2/ **The Logic & Algorithm**: Emphasize the core processing part (e.g., how you use your data tools to extract insights from complex, messy datasets). Don't just list programming languages. explain why your team's approach is smart, optimized for speed, or highly accurate.
#### 3/ **Tech-to-Value Translation**: Conclude this phase by affirming that your technical backend is the exact engine driving the smooth, practical results they just saw in the demo
---
## **PHASE 3: Business Model & Viability (~2 minutes)**
#### 1/ **Target Audience (User and Customer)**: Clearly define who uses the product (User) and who actually pays for it (Customer). Keep this highly realistic and grounded
#### 2/ **Monetization Strategy**: Explain how money flows into the project. Define a clear model: Subscription, Pay-per-use, Freemium, or a B2B model (licensing data/API to other businesses)
#### 3/ **Competitive Advantage (the Moat)**: quickly answer: "Why haven't big tech companies done this yet, or if they have, what is your team's unique edge?" (e.g., Niche market focus, deployment speed, lower operational costs)
---
## **PHASE 4: Future Roadmap (~30 seconds)**
#### 1/ **Current Milestone**: Acknowledge that this is currently an MVP (Minimum Viable Product) built within the strict 2 days hackathon timeframe
#### 2/ **Next ... Months**: Present 2-3 specific, actionable next steps (e.g., transitioning from a Web platform to a Native Mobile App, integrating Machine Learning for automation, or scaling from local to city-wide deployment)
---
## **PHASE 5: The Wrap-up & Ending (~30 seconds)**
#### 1/ **Full Circle**: Bring the presentation back to the exact statistic or "pain point" you mentioned in the very first second of the pitch.The Vision Statement: Deliver an inspiring punchline about the core value and long-term impact your project brings to society or the industry
#### 2/ **A clean, professional sign-off: "And we are team <Team name>. Thank you everybody for your time and attention."**
