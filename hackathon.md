#### Team Greeting Template:"Good [morning/afternoon] judges and everyone, we are team [Team Name]. Today, we are here to tackle a critical issue in [Target Industry/Field].
---
## PHASE 1: The Hook, Problem & Demo (1.5 minutes)
#### 1/ **The Hook (Stats/Fact)**: Start with 1-2 shocking statistics or a pressing issue to grab the judges' attention in the very first seconds
#### 2/ **The Solution Abstract**: Briefly state your team's solution (e.g., "We built a platform to detect and analyze...")
#### 3/ **The Demo**: Introduce a real-world scenario (User Story) and jump straight into the live demo. Show the judges exactly how users interact with the product to solve the stated problem.PHASE 2: Under the Hood - The Secret Sauce (1.5 minutes)Tech Stack Overview: Present the system architecture using a simple flow chart (Data flow $\rightarrow$ Processing $\rightarrow$ UI).The Logic & Algorithm: Emphasize the core processing part (e.g., how you use your data tools to extract insights from complex, messy datasets). Don't just list programming languages; explain why your team's approach is smart, optimized for speed, or highly accurate.Tech-to-Value Translation: Conclude this phase by affirming that your technical backend is the exact engine driving the smooth, practical results they just saw in the demo.PHASE 3: Business Model & Viability (1 minute)Target Audience (User vs. Customer): Clearly define who uses the product (User) and who actually pays for it (Customer). Keep this highly realistic and grounded.Monetization Strategy: Explain how money flows into the project. Define a clear model: Subscription, Pay-per-use, Freemium, or a B2B model (licensing data/API to other businesses).Competitive Advantage (The Moat): Quickly answer: "Why haven't big tech companies done this yet, or if they have, what is your team's unique edge?" (e.g., Niche market focus, deployment speed, lower operational costs).PHASE 4: Future Roadmap (0.5 minutes)Current Milestone: Acknowledge that this is currently an MVP (Minimum Viable Product) built within the strict 1-month hackathon timeframe.Next 6-12 Months: Present 2-3 specific, actionable next steps (e.g., transitioning from a Web platform to a Native Mobile App, integrating Machine Learning for automation, or scaling from local to city-wide deployment).PHASE 5: The Wrap-up & Ending (0.5 minutes)Full Circle: Bring the presentation back to the exact statistic or "pain point" you mentioned in the very first second of the pitch.The Vision Statement: Deliver an inspiring punchline about the core value and long-term impact your project brings to society or the industry.Call to Action: A clean, professional sign-off: "Thank you for your time and attention. We are team [Team Name], and we are now open for your questions."
