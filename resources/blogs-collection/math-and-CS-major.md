## 5 PROGRAMS – 5 PATHWAYS TO THE FUTURE

### Mathematics

Designed for students who are passionate about mathematical reasoning, proof, research, teaching, and fundamental sciences.

### Mathematics and Computer Science

Combining Mathematics and Computer Science, this program provides knowledge in algorithms, programming, data science, artificial intelligence, and optimization. Students can pursue careers in software, AI, data, and high-tech industries.

### Applied Mathematics

Applying Mathematics to solve real-world problems in finance, engineering, mathematical modeling, and optimization.

### Data Science

One of the most promising fields today, this program provides in-depth training in statistics, machine learning, AI, and data mining. Graduates can work in finance, e-commerce, biomedical sciences, insurance, and technology.

### Statistics

Providing a strong foundation in probability, statistical modeling, and data analysis for applications in forecasting, risk management, and decision-making.

## WHY DO COMPANIES NEED PEOPLE WHO ARE GOOD AT MATHEMATICS?

AI, Machine Learning, Data Science, Fintech, optimization, mathematical modeling, big data analytics, and many other fields are built upon mathematical foundations.

Studying Mathematics is not simply about learning formulas. It teaches students how to think, analyze, prove, model, and solve complex problems. These are capabilities that remain valuable even as technology continues to evolve.

In the AI era, knowing how to use tools is an advantage. But **understanding the mathematical foundations behind those tools is a more sustainable advantage.**
